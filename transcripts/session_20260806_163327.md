Answer: Northbay contracts do not include a general “termination‑for‑convenience” right once the evaluation period ends. A customer can only end the agreement early if the other party commits an uncured material breach; in that case either side may give 30 days’ written notice and an opportunity to cure before termination takes effect. If a customer wants to leave before the contract term expires for any other reason, they must contact their Northbay account team to discuss possible options.  

When a termination occurs, Northbay keeps a copy of the customer’s configuration and source‑mapping data for 30 days to support any potential re‑activation, after which all customer data stored in Northbay‑managed storage is permanently deleted. Data that resides in the customer’s own cloud account is not affected, because Northbay Studio runs inside that account.
